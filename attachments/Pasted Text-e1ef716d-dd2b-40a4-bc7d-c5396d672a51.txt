4. Words we use (glossary — short)
One screen, one line each:
- Chance it hits — how often we think this ticket wins.
- Book — chance baked into Hard Rock’s price.
- Edge — our chance minus the book’s chance.
- Strong / Fair / Thin look — how complete the information is. Not the chance it hits.
- Photo to lock this price — until you photograph Hard Rock, the dollars may be off.
- The Call — tonight’s one strict pre-game pick. Not a guarantee.
- Parlay — two or more picks that all have to hit.
- Same-game — two picks from one game. They move together.
- Live — the game already started.
- Log — where photographed tickets wait, then you tap Hit or Miss.
- This site never places a bet.
